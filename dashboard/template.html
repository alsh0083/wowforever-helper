Specs by archetype

Leveling builds: each endgame build on its way to 60. Its own talents in an order chosen for questing pace, plus a few leveling-only talents where they pay off (respecced at 60). PvP builds pick up their PvP talents early. Percentages compare average questing pace with the best in the column.

Percentages compare each build with the best one in its column (100% = best; hover a number for what goes into it). Community standards come first; model picks are what the calculator found and have not been tried in game.
