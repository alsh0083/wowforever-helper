WoW Forever Priest abilities: what changed outside the talents

3 abilities

Some of the biggest Priest changes are trained spells rather than talents. These 3 notes come from the level 60 Beta spellbook; each one links to the talents that reference it. Ranks and levels can still change before launch.

Shadow Word: DeathNew

An execute-style Shadow spell: 30 yd, instant, 15 sec cooldown. If the target lives, you take backlash equal to 10% of your maximum health. Four ranks, first trained at 32. Early Demise refers to it.

How to plan WoW Forever Priest talents

Plan WoW Forever Priest talents around Mana first. Meditation now starts at 17% regeneration while casting and reaches 50% at rank 3, Litany of Light pays you for alternating heals, and Shadowform halves the cost of Shadow spells. Decide whether you heal, shield or channel Mind Flay, then trace that tree's prerequisites backwards.

Set your level and budget

Level first: WoW Forever Priest talents come from one pool of 21 points at level 30 and 51 at level 60, shared by all three trees. Power Infusion, Prayer of Mending and Shadowform each need 30 points in earlier rows of their tree, so a 51-point plan reaches one of them with 20 points to spare; a 21-point plan reaches none.

Learn and refund ranks

Click to learn a rank; a right-click or Shift-click refunds it. On a touchscreen, tap to inspect, then tap again to learn. Locked WoW Forever Priest talents show what they wait for: Penance needs 20 Discipline points, Binding Heal 15 in Holy, and Mind Flay 10 in Shadow. Keyboard users move with Tab and confirm with Enter or Space.

Reach the later rows

Each later row wants 5 points already spent in earlier rows of the same tree for every row after the first, so later WoW Forever Priest talents are gated by their own tree. Shadow spreads its new talents: Improved Mind Flay in row three, Devouring Contagion in row five, Early Demise in row six. Refund dependants first when you back out, or reset the tree.

Save and share WoW Forever Priest talents

A Priest keeps more than one plan: a Discipline shield spread, a Holy healing layout, a Shadow build for solo play. Your current allocation of WoW Forever Priest talents saves as one draft; open My builds, name a setup such as Shadow 31/20 and choose Save build to keep several. Names stay on this device.

Share WoW Forever Priest talents as a link with Copy build; it holds class, level, every rank and data version, so a friend opens your exact Penance or Shadowform path. When the clipboard refuses, select the link yourself; devices with a share sheet also show Share. Saves are local only, so keep a link before clearing browser data.

WoW Forever Priest talents FAQ

Answers for preview changes, source coverage and saved setups.

How do WoW Forever Priest talents differ from Classic?+

In this preview, 13 Priest talents are new and 31 changed. Shadow Weaving is now a stacking buff on you rather than a debuff on the target, and Shadowform grants 10% Shadow damage instead of 15% but halves Shadow spell costs. Compare to Classic puts the old text beside the new.

Are all talent ranks verified?+

This snapshot of WoW Forever Priest talents has 148 recorded rank descriptions and 0 slots without one. Holy Specialization, Spell Warding, Divine Fury and Blackout, plus Mental Strength, Spiritual Guidance and Darkness, now have every rank written. The full reference still marks any later gap.

Can I restore WoW Forever Priest talents on another device?+

Yes, by link. Open the copied build link in the other browser and it restores class, level and every point, whether a 31-point Shadow plan or a Holy layout. Drafts and named saves live only in the browser that saved them. A link from an older data version explains the difference and keeps its address.

Are these the best WoW Forever Priest talents?+

No. The calculator confirms budget, row gates and prerequisites, nothing more; it does not simulate healing or damage. Compare WoW Forever Priest talents by reading the recorded effects: Spiritual Guidance scales with Spirit, Renewed Hope needs Weakened Soul on the target. Test the result in game.
