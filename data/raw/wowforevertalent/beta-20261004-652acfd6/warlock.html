How to plan WoW Forever Warlock talents

Plan WoW Forever Warlock talents around the demon you keep out. Demonic Knowledge, Demonic Energies and Master Demonologist only work with a summoned demon, while Demonic Sacrifice trades the pet for a two-hour buff and Demonic Pact lets you resummon a different one without losing it. Decide that first, then trace the tree's prerequisites backwards.

Set your level and budget

Level sets the budget for WoW Forever Warlock talents: 21 points at level 30, 51 at level 60, and the three trees draw from the same total. Wrack, Demonic Pact and Incinerate each need 30 points in earlier rows of their tree, so a 51-point plan reaches one of them with 20 points to spare; a 21-point plan reaches none.

Learn and refund ranks

Click to learn a rank, right-click or Shift-click to refund it; touchscreens inspect on the first tap and learn on the second. Locked WoW Forever Warlock talents show what they wait for: Siphon Life needs 20 Affliction points, Demonic Sacrifice 10 in Demonology, and Conflagrate 15 in Destruction. From a keyboard, Tab and Enter or Space do the same.

Reach the later rows

Rows unlock in order: 5 points in earlier rows of the same tree for row two, 10 for row three, so later WoW Forever Warlock talents are gated by their own tree. Destruction stacks its new talents late: Bane of Havoc and Fire and Brimstone in row five, Shadow and Flame in row six. Refund dependants ahead of their prerequisite, or reset the tree.

Save and share WoW Forever Warlock talents

A Warlock keeps more than one plan: an Affliction drain spread, a Demonology pet layout, a Destruction build around Conflagrate and Incinerate. Your current allocation of WoW Forever Warlock talents saves as one draft; open My builds, name a setup such as Demo 31/20 and choose Save build to keep several. Names stay on this device.

Copy build shares WoW Forever Warlock talents as a link that stores class, level, every rank and data version, so a friend opens your Wrack or Incinerate path. If automatic copying fails, select the link in the field; Share shows up on devices that offer it. Local saves do not sync, so keep a link before clearing browser data.

WoW Forever Warlock talents FAQ

Answers for preview changes, source coverage and saved setups.

How do WoW Forever Warlock talents differ from Classic?+

This preview lists 18 Warlock talents as new and 31 as changed. Ruin adds 20% critical damage instead of 100%, Curse of Exhaustion slows by 30% rather than 10%, and Demonic Sacrifice lasts two hours instead of thirty minutes. Compare to Classic shows the old text beside the new one.

Are all talent ranks verified?+

This snapshot of WoW Forever Warlock talents currently has 149 recorded rank descriptions and 0 rank slots with none. Improved Life Tap, Improved Voidwalker, Improved Sayaad and Master Summoner, plus Suppression, Ruin and Master Demonologist, now have every rank written. The full reference still marks any later gap.

Can I restore WoW Forever Warlock talents on another device?+

Yes. Open the copied build link in the other browser to restore class, level and every point, whether a 31-point Affliction plan or a Destruction layout. Drafts and named saves remain on the device that made them. A link from an older data version shows an explanation and keeps its address.

Are these the best WoW Forever Warlock talents?+

No. The calculator checks only budget, row gates and prerequisites; it does not simulate damage. Compare WoW Forever Warlock talents by reading the recorded effects: Improved Drains scales with your other Affliction effects, Bane of Havoc with extra targets. Test the finished build in game before you trust it.
