WoW Forever Paladin abilities: what changed outside the talents

4 abilities

Some of the biggest Paladin changes are trained spells rather than talents. These 4 notes come from the level 60 Beta spellbook; each one links to the talents that reference it. Ranks and levels can still change before launch.

JudgementChanged

No longer consumes your seal. 10 yd range, 10 sec cooldown. Judging Seal of Fury taunts the target for 4 sec, so Paladins can pick up pulls.

Swift Judgement, Improved Judgement, Sanctified Judgement

Seal of FuryNew

A tanking seal: every hit adds Holy damage, and with a shield equipped you gain an absorb worth half of that damage. Seven ranks from level 10 to 58.

Improved Seal of Fury

Holy StrikeNew

A baseline Retribution strike on a 10 sec cooldown dealing weapon damage plus Holy damage. Eight ranks from level 6 to 60. Several talents build on it.

Iron Creed, Sacred Arbiter

Blessing of Kings and ConsecrationNow trainable

Both are trainable now instead of talents; Consecration is learned at level 20.

Consecration, Blessing of Kings

How to plan WoW Forever Paladin talents

Plan WoW Forever Paladin talents around Judgement and the seal you keep up. Judgement no longer consumes the seal, Swift Judgement resets its cooldown, Sanctified Judgement returns Mana with it, and Twist of Light rewards changing seals. Decide whether you heal, tank or swing a two-hander, then trace that tree's prerequisites backwards.

Set your level and budget

Pick the level before placing WoW Forever Paladin talents: 21 points at level 30, 51 at level 60, one pool for all three trees. Light's Vigil, Holy Shield and Twist of Light each need 30 points in earlier rows of their tree, so a 51-point plan reaches one of them with 20 to spare; Twist of Light also requires level 40.

Learn and refund ranks

A click learns a rank; right-click or Shift-click takes it back; on touch, the first tap inspects and the second learns. Locked WoW Forever Paladin talents show what they wait for: a shield for Holy Shield, 20 Holy points for Holy Shock, 20 Protection points for Templar's Bulwark. From a keyboard, Tab reaches a node; Enter or Space learns it.

Reach the later rows

Row two needs 5 points already spent in earlier rows of the same tree, row three needs 10, and so on, so later WoW Forever Paladin talents are gated by their tree. Retribution keeps new talents late: Sacred Arbiter in row four, Champion of the Light and Instrument of Law in row six. Arrows mark full-rank prerequisites; refund dependants first, or reset the tree.

Save and share WoW Forever Paladin talents

A Paladin often keeps three plans: a Holy healing spread, a Protection shield build around Templar's Bulwark, a Retribution two-hander build. Your current allocation of WoW Forever Paladin talents saves as one draft; open My builds, name a setup such as Prot 31/20 and choose Save build to keep several. Names stay on this device.

Copy build is how you share WoW Forever Paladin talents. The link stores class, level, every rank and data version, so a friend opens your Light's Vigil or Holy Shield path. If the clipboard is blocked, select the link yourself; a Share button appears where supported. Local saves never leave the browser; keep a link before clearing site data.

WoW Forever Paladin talents FAQ

Answers for preview changes, source coverage and saved setups.

How do WoW Forever Paladin talents differ from Classic?+

18 Paladin talents are marked new in this preview and 23 changed. Improved Righteous Fury now cuts damage taken by 2% instead of adding threat, and Eye for an Eye answers every critical strike, not just spells. Turn on Compare to Classic to see the old wording and any moved positions.

Are all talent ranks verified?+

This snapshot of WoW Forever Paladin talents records 138 rank descriptions and leaves 0 slots empty. Divine Strength, Divine Intellect, Toughness and Deflection, plus Redoubt, Reckoning and Iron Creed, now have every rank written. The full reference still marks any later gap.

Can I restore WoW Forever Paladin talents on another device?+

Yes, through a build link: opened in the other browser, it restores class, level and every point, whether a 31-point Holy plan or a Protection layout. Drafts and named saves live only in the browser that created them. A link made on an older data version explains what changed and keeps its address.

Are these the best WoW Forever Paladin talents?+

No ranking is implied. The calculator verifies budget, row gates and prerequisites; it does not simulate healing, threat or damage. Compare WoW Forever Paladin talents by reading the recorded effects: Champion of the Light scales with Intellect, Vengeance with critical strikes. Try the finished build in game before you call it best.
