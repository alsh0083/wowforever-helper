How to plan WoW Forever Rogue talents

Plan WoW Forever Rogue talents around your weapons and your finishers. Daggers raise Hemorrhage to 145% and Ghostly Strike to 180% weapon damage, Hack and Slash pays out by weapon type, and Flawless Execution cuts the Energy cost of Eviscerate. Choose the tree you will invest most points in, then trace its prerequisites backwards.

Set your level and budget

Start with the level; it caps WoW Forever Rogue talents at 21 points for level 30 and 51 for level 60, one pool for all three trees. Venom, Adrenaline Rush and Thousand Cuts each need 30 points in earlier rows of their tree, so a 51-point plan reaches one of them with 20 points to spare; a 21-point plan reaches none.

Learn and refund ranks

Left-click to learn a rank, right-click or Shift-click to refund one; on touchscreens the first tap inspects and the second learns. Locked WoW Forever Rogue talents show what they wait for: Mutilate needs 20 Assassination points, Blade Flurry 20 in Combat, and Hemorrhage 20 in Subtlety. Tab focuses a node for keyboard users, and Enter or Space learns it.

Reach the later rows

Five cumulative points in earlier rows of the same tree unlock row two, ten unlock row three, and so on, so later WoW Forever Rogue talents answer to their own tree only. Subtlety keeps its new talents late: Quietus and Cutthroat sit in row six, Thousand Cuts in row seven. Undo in order, dependants before prerequisites, or reset the tree in one go.

Save and share WoW Forever Rogue talents

A Rogue keeps more than one plan: a poison-heavy Assassination spread, a sword-and-axe Combat layout, a dagger Subtlety build for Hemorrhage. Your current allocation of WoW Forever Rogue talents saves as one draft; open My builds, name a setup such as Combat 31/20 and choose Save build to keep several. Names stay on this device.

Copy build turns WoW Forever Rogue talents into a link. It carries class, level, every rank and data version, so a friend opens your exact Mutilate or Hemorrhage path. If copying fails, select the text in the field; a Share button appears on devices that support sharing. Local saves stay behind when you clear browser data, so keep a link.

WoW Forever Rogue talents FAQ

Answers for preview changes, source coverage and saved setups.

How do WoW Forever Rogue talents differ from Classic?+

This preview marks 9 of the Rogue talents new and 31 changed. Improved Expose Armor cuts Energy cost and refunds combo points instead of stripping armor, Vigor gives 5 Energy rather than 10, and Dirty Deeds lets Garrote open from the front. Compare to Classic shows the old text and any moved positions.

Are all talent ranks verified?+

This snapshot of WoW Forever Rogue talents currently carries 139 recorded rank descriptions with 0 slots missing. Vile Poisons, Seal Fate and Lightning Reflexes, plus Malice, Lethality and Quietus, now have every rank written. The full reference still marks any later gap.

Can I restore WoW Forever Rogue talents on another device?+

Yes. Paste the copied build link into the other browser and it restores class, level and every point, whether a 31-point Combat plan or a Subtlety layout. Drafts and named saves stay on the device that made them; nothing syncs. An older link shows an explanation and keeps its address so you can still copy it.

Are these the best WoW Forever Rogue talents?+

No. Budget, row gates and prerequisites are all the calculator checks; it does not simulate damage or Energy flow. Compare WoW Forever Rogue talents by reading the recorded effects: Mutilate wants poisoned targets, Hack and Slash wants the right weapon type. The finished build still needs testing in game.
