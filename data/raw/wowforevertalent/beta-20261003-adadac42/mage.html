WoW Forever Mage abilities: what changed outside the talents

2 abilities

Some of the biggest Mage changes are trained spells rather than talents. These 2 notes come from the level 60 Beta spellbook; each one links to the talents that reference it. Ranks and levels can still change before launch.

Comprehend ScrollNew

A small utility spell that deciphers an untranslated scroll, seen on a Skyborne Mage.

Frostfire BoltNew

A 3 sec bolt that counts as both Fire and Frost: it deals Frostfire damage, slows movement by 40% and applies a 9 sec Frostfire DoT. Three ranks, first trained at 40.

How to plan WoW Forever Mage talents

Plan WoW Forever Mage talents around Frozen targets. Ice Lance deals 300% more damage to a Frozen target, Shatter now adds 17% critical chance against one and Fingers of Frost fakes the state for a cast, while each Arcane Blast raises your other spells' damage by 10%. Pick your main tree and trace its prerequisites backwards.

Set your level and budget

Decide the level before you spend WoW Forever Mage talents: 21 points at level 30, 51 at level 60, and all three trees share that budget. Arcane Power, Combustion and Ice Barrier each need 30 points in earlier rows of their tree, so a 51-point plan reaches one of them with 20 points to spare; a 21-point plan reaches none.

Learn and refund ranks

Clicking learns a rank, right-clicking or Shift-clicking refunds it, and on a touchscreen the first tap inspects while the second learns. Locked WoW Forever Mage talents show what they wait for: Arcane Blast needs 10 Arcane points, Heating Up 15 in Fire, and Fingers of Frost 20 in Frost. Keyboard: Tab to a node, then Enter or Space.

Reach the later rows

A row unlocks after 5 points in earlier rows of the same tree for every row after the first, so later WoW Forever Mage talents are gated by their own tree. Frost keeps its cooldowns mid-tree: Ice Block in row four, Cold Snap in row five, and Ice Barrier in row seven. Back out by refunding dependants before their prerequisite, or reset the tree.

Save and share WoW Forever Mage talents

A Mage keeps more than one plan: an Arcane Blast spread, an Ignite Fire layout, a Frost build for Ice Lance. Your current allocation of WoW Forever Mage talents saves as one draft; open My builds, name a setup such as Frost 31/20 and choose Save build to keep several. Names stay on this device.

Use Copy build to share WoW Forever Mage talents; the link stores class, level, every rank and data version, so a friend opens your Heating Up or Ice Lance path. If the clipboard says no, select the link in the field; Share appears where the device supports it. Keep a link before clearing browser data; local saves do not sync.

WoW Forever Mage talents FAQ

Answers for preview changes, source coverage and saved setups.

How do WoW Forever Mage talents differ from Classic?+

The preview flags 6 Mage talents as new and 31 as changed. Arcane Mind now grants 2% Intellect and 20% Arcane critical damage instead of 2% Mana, and Improved Counterspell always silences for 2 sec rather than half the time for 4. Compare to Classic shows the previous text beside the new.

Are all talent ranks verified?+

This snapshot of WoW Forever Mage talents holds 146 recorded rank descriptions, and 0 rank slots have none. Arcane Concentration, Ignite and Improved Frostbolt, plus Arcane Focus, Improved Fireball and Winter's Chill, now have every rank written. The full reference still marks any later gap.

Can I restore WoW Forever Mage talents on another device?+

Yes. Open the copied build link on the other device and it restores class, level and every point, whether a 31-point Frost plan or an Arcane layout. Drafts and named saves stay in the browser that made them. An older link explains the version change and keeps its address available.

Are these the best WoW Forever Mage talents?+

No. Budget, row gates and prerequisites are the only checks; the calculator does not simulate damage or Mana. Compare WoW Forever Mage talents by reading the recorded effects: Heating Up and Missile Barrage only trigger from the spells they name. Test the finished build in game before deciding.
