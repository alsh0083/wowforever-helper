Mage talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Mage 49 talents. Forever keeps 47 of them (11 word for word, 26 with a rewritten tooltip, 8 with the same sentence and different numbers and 2 differing only in capitalisation or punctuation) and renames 7, changes the rank count of 8, adds 6 new talents and leaves 2 out of the trees. Arcane moved most: 3 new, 12 changed, 1 gone and 3 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Arcane 3 new · 12 changed · 3 same · 1 gone
  • Fire 1 new · 11 changed · 5 same · 0 gone
  • Frost 2 new · 13 changed · 3 same · 1 gone

New Mage talents in Forever (6)

  • Missile Barrage Arcane 5 points 2 ranks

    Gives your Arcane Blast spell a 20% chance, and your Arcane Wrath, Fireball, Frostbolt, Frost Blast, and Frostfire Bolt spells a 10% chance to reduce the channeled duration of your next Arcane Missiles spell by 50%, reduce the Mana cost by 100%, and missiles fire every 0.5 sec.

  • Arcane Blast Arcane 10 points 1 rank

    Blasts the target with energy, dealing 33 to 39 Arcane damage. Each time you cast Arcane Blast, you gain 12% of your Intellect as Spell Damage and the mana cost of Arcane Blast is increased by 100%. Effect stacks up to 4 times and lasts 18 sec from time of initial application.

  • Arcane Wrath Arcane 15 points 1 rank

    Launches a projectile of pure Arcane energy at the enemy target, causing 121 to 147 Arcane damage. This spell gains additional Spell Damage equal to 5% of your maximum Mana.

  • Heating Up Fire 15 points 1 rank

    Non-periodic critical strikes with Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast, and Scorch reduce the cast time of your next Pyroblast cast within 20 sec by 25%, stacking up to 3 times. Fire Blast grants an additional stack every time it deals damage.

  • Ice Lance Frost 10 points 1 rank

    Deals 28 to 33 Frost damage to an enemy target. Deals 300% increased damage to Frozen targets.

  • Fingers of Frost Frost 20 points 2 ranks

    Gives your Chill effects a 15% chance to grant you the Fingers of Frost effect, which treats your next 1 spell cast as if the target were Frozen. Lasts 15 sec.

Questions about Mage talent changes

How many Mage talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 49 Mage talents in Classic Era, 11 read exactly the same in Forever, 36 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 2 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 6 new Mage talents, for 53 in all.
What are the new Mage talents in WoW Forever?
6 Mage talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Missile Barrage, Arcane Blast, Arcane Wrath, Heating Up, Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost.
Which Classic Mage talents are not in WoW Forever?
2 Classic Era Mage talents are not in the Forever trees: Arcane Focus and Piercing Ice.
Which Mage tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Arcane: 3 new talents, 12 changed and 1 gone from the tree, against 3 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Mage talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
7 Mage talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Improved Channeling (was Improved Arcane Missiles), Arcane Geometry (was Magic Attunement), Arcane Impact (was Improved Arcane Explosion), Arcane Shielding (was Improved Mana Shield), Incineration (was Incinerate), Accelerant (was Improved Fireball) and Precision (was Elemental Precision).
