Druid talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Druid 47 talents. Forever keeps 38 of them (5 word for word, 31 with a rewritten tooltip and 2 with the same sentence and different numbers) and renames 5, changes the rank count of 7, adds 14 new talents and leaves 8 out of the trees (2 of those are taught by trainers instead). Feral Combat moved most: 7 new, 13 changed, 2 gone and 0 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Balance 4 new · 12 changed · 0 same · 4 gone
  • Feral Combat 7 new · 13 changed · 0 same · 2 gone
  • Restoration 3 new · 8 changed · 5 same · 2 gone

New Druid talents in Forever (14)

  • Genesis Balance 5 ranks

    Increases the periodic damage and healing done by your spells and abilities by 2%.

  • Nature's Majesty Balance 5 points 2 ranks

    Increases your critical strike chance with spells and melee attacks by 2%.

  • Nature's Splendor Balance 10 points 1 rank

    Increases the duration of your Moonfire and Rejuvenation spells by 3 sec, your Regrowth spell by 6 sec, and your Insect Swarm spell by 2 sec.

  • Eclipse Balance 20 points 3 ranks

    Your Wrath spell reduces the cast time of your next 2 Starfire spells by 0.17 sec. Stores up to 4 charges. Lasts 15 sec.

  • Lacerate Feral Combat 15 points 1 rank

    Lacerates the enemy target, making them bleed for 20 damage over 15 sec plus 10% weapon damage per existing application of Lacerate on the target. Causes a high amount of threat. This effect stacks up to 5 times on the same target.

  • Predatory Discipline Feral Combat 15 points 1 rank

    Your Ferocious Bite ability no longer converts extra Energy into damage, but still deals extra damage based on your excess Energy.

  • Natural Instinct Feral Combat 20 points 2 ranks

    Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your melee abilities by 10% and increases your spell healing by 12% of your Intellect.

  • Rend and Tear Feral Combat 25 points 5 ranks

    Increases damage done by your melee abilities on Bleeding targets by 2%.

  • Natural Reaction Feral Combat 25 points 5 ranks

    Increases your dodge chance by 1%, and gives you a 20% chance to gain 5 Rage each time you dodge.

  • Primal Bite Feral Combat 30 points 1 rank

    Bite the target, dealing 100% normal damage plus 42 and generating a high amount of threat.

  • Shifting Power Feral Combat 30 points 1 rank

    Instantly convert 55% of Base Mana into 40 Energy. Shifting Power's cost is reduced by effects that reduce the cost of Shapeshifting.

  • Bloom Restoration 10 points 1 rank

    When your Rejuvenation or Regrowth spell expires due to its duration, the target is instantly healed for an amount equal to 100% of your Spirit. If either spell expires due to being dispelled, the target is healed for 50% of your Spirit instead.

  • Living Spirit Restoration 20 points 3 ranks

    Increases your Spirit by 5%.

  • Wild Growth Restoration 30 points 1 rank

    Heals the target and their party for 336 over 7 sec. Party members must be within 43.5 yards of target. The amount healed is applied quickly at first, and slows down as Wild Growth reaches its full duration.

Questions about Druid talent changes

How many Druid talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 47 Druid talents in Classic Era, 5 read exactly the same in Forever, 33 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 8 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 14 new Druid talents, for 52 in all.
What are the new Druid talents in WoW Forever?
14 Druid talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Genesis, Nature's Majesty, Nature's Splendor, Eclipse, Lacerate, Predatory Discipline, Natural Instinct, Rend and Tear, Natural Reaction, Primal Bite, Shifting Power, Bloom, Living Spirit and Wild Growth.
Which Classic Druid talents are not in WoW Forever?
8 Classic Era Druid talents are not in the Forever trees: Improved Thorns, Natural Weapons, Nature's Grasp, Omen of Clarity, Faerie Fire (Feral), Feral Aggression, Improved Enrage and Improved Mark of the Wild. Nature's Grasp and Faerie Fire (Feral) are taught by trainers instead.
Which Druid tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Feral Combat: 7 new talents, 13 changed and 2 gone from the tree, against 0 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Druid talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
5 Druid talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Overgrowth (was Improved Nature's Grasp), Feral Swiftness (was Feline Swiftness), Shredding Attacks (was Improved Shred), Blood Frenzy (was Primal Fury) and Naturalist (was Improved Healing Touch).
