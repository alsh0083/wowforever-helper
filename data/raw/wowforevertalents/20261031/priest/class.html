Priest talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Priest 47 talents. Forever keeps 38 of them (8 word for word, 23 with a rewritten tooltip, 6 with the same sentence and different numbers and 1 differing only in capitalisation or punctuation) and renames 1, changes the rank count of 7, adds 14 new talents and leaves 9 out of the trees (1 of them is taught by trainers instead). Discipline moved most: 7 new, 11 changed, 4 gone and 0 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Discipline 7 new · 11 changed · 0 same · 4 gone
  • Holy 3 new · 10 changed · 4 same · 2 gone
  • Shadow Magic 4 new · 9 changed · 4 same · 3 gone

New Priest talents in Forever (14)

  • Power in Light Discipline 5 ranks

    Your Smite and Penance spells deal 2% increased damage to targets afflicted with your Holy Fire or Shadow Word: Pain.

  • Twin Disciplines Discipline 5 ranks

    Increases the damage and healing of your instant cast spells by 1%.

  • Holy Precision Discipline 5 points 3 ranks

    Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 6%.

  • Soul Warding Discipline 15 points 1 rank

    Reduces the cooldown on your Power Word: Shield spell by 4 sec and reduces its mana cost by 15%.

  • Penance Discipline 20 points 1 rank

    Launches a volley of holy light at the target, causing 45 Holy damage to an enemy, or 134 healing to an ally, instantly and every 1 sec for 2 sec.

  • Renewed Hope Discipline 20 points 5 ranks

    Your heals from Flash Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, and Penance gain 2% increased critical strike chance when cast on targets with Weakened Soul, and reduce the remaining duration of Weakened Soul on their target by 1 sec.

  • Divine Aegis Discipline 25 points 3 ranks

    Your non-periodic critical heals create a protective shield on the target, absorbing 5% of the amount healed. Shields up to 1200 damage and lasts 12 sec.

  • Holy Binding Holy 15 points 1 rank

    Each time you heal a target other than yourself with Flash Heal, you are healed for 50% as much. This healing generates no threat.

  • Litany of Light Holy 20 points 2 ranks

    When you cast a healing spell, gain Mana equal to 5% of the base cost of the spell if your previous heal was a different spell.

  • Prayer of Mending Holy 30 points 1 rank

    Places a spell on the target that heals them for (172 + 42.9% of healing power) the next time they take damage or receive non-periodic healing. When the heal occurs, Prayer of Mending jumps to a party or raid member within 20 yards. Jumps up to 5 times and lasts 30 sec after each jump. This spell can only be placed on one target at a time per caster.

  • Improved Mind Flay Shadow Magic 10 points 2 ranks

    Each instance of damage of Mind Flay deals 10% more damage than its previous instance of damage.

  • Dark Inspiration Shadow Magic 15 points 5 ranks

    Your Mind Blast and fully channeled Mind Flay causes Dark Inspiration, increasing yours and your party's Spirit by 3% for 10 sec.

  • Devouring Contagion Shadow Magic 20 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the mana cost of your Devouring Plague by 25%. Targets that die while Devouring Plague is active spreads it, jumping to 2 nearby enemies within 5 yds for the remaining duration.

  • Early Demise Shadow Magic 25 points 2 ranks

    Increases Shadow Word: Death's critical strike chance on targets at or below 20% health by 15%.

Questions about Priest talent changes

How many Priest talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 47 Priest talents in Classic Era, 8 read exactly the same in Forever, 30 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 9 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 14 new Priest talents, for 52 in all.
What are the new Priest talents in WoW Forever?
14 Priest talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Power in Light, Twin Disciplines, Holy Precision, Soul Warding, Penance, Renewed Hope, Divine Aegis, Holy Binding, Litany of Light, Prayer of Mending, Improved Mind Flay, Dark Inspiration, Devouring Contagion and Early Demise.
Which Classic Priest talents are not in WoW Forever?
9 Classic Era Priest talents are not in the Forever trees: Divine Spirit, Force of Will, Improved Power Word: Fortitude, Unbreakable Will, Improved Prayer of Healing, Lightwell, Improved Fade, Improved Vampiric Embrace and Shadow Affinity. Divine Spirit is taught by trainers instead.
Which Priest tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Discipline: 7 new talents, 11 changed and 4 gone from the tree, against 0 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Priest talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
1 Priest talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Twilight Focus (was Healing Focus).
