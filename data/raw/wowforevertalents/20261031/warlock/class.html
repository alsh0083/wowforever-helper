Warlock talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Warlock 50 talents. Forever keeps 35 of them (7 word for word, 23 with a rewritten tooltip, 4 with the same sentence and different numbers and 1 differing only in capitalisation or punctuation) and renames 4, changes the rank count of 8, adds 17 new talents and leaves 15 out of the trees (1 of them is taught by trainers instead). Demonology moved most: 7 new, 9 changed, 5 gone and 3 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Affliction 5 new · 9 changed · 3 same · 5 gone
  • Demonology 7 new · 9 changed · 3 same · 5 gone
  • Destruction 5 new · 10 changed · 1 same · 5 gone

New Warlock talents in Forever (17)

  • Soul Harvest Affliction 5 points 2 ranks

    Killing a non-trivial target afflicted by your Drain Soul increases your Mana regeneration by 50% for 10 sec and allows 50% of normal Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

  • Improved Drains Affliction 5 points 3 ranks

    Increases Health drained, damage done, or Mana drained by your Drain Life, Drain Soul, and Drain Mana spells by 7%.

  • Pandemic Affliction 10 points 3 ranks

    Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Corruption, Bane of Agony, Bane of Doom, Drain Soul, Drain Life, Siphon Life, and Wrack spells by 33%.

  • Malevolence Affliction 15 points 5 ranks

    Increases the critical effect chance of your Shadow spells by 1%.

  • Wrack Affliction 30 points 1 rank

    Tears the target apart from within, inflicting 36 Shadow damage every 1 sec and increasing the damage they take from your other Shadow damage over time effects by 10% for 6 sec.

  • Demonic Aegis Demonology 5 points 2 ranks

    Increases the effectiveness of your Demon Skin and Demon Armor spells by 15%.

  • Demonic Energies Demonology 5 points 2 ranks

    You heal your pet for 8% of all spell damage you deal. When you gain Mana from Life Tap, your summoned demon gains 50% of the Mana you gain.

  • Decimation Demonology 15 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the cooldown of your Soul Fire spell by 45%. When you cast Shadow Bolt or Searing Pain on an enemy below 35% health, they deal 3% increased damage, and for the next 10 sec your Soul Fire spell has its cast time reduced by 20% and costs no Soul Shards.

  • Demonic Brand Demonology 15 points 3 ranks

    Your Searing Pain generates 17% less threat and brands the target for 10 sec. Your pet's next 2 attacks against the target deal (65 + 7.8% of the higher of Fire and Shadow Spell Power) to (68 + 7.8% of the higher of Fire and Shadow Spell Power) Fire or Shadow damage based on the pet. If Shadow damage is dealt, it has high threat.

  • Improved Felhunter Demonology 20 points 3 ranks

    Increases the Attack Power reduction of your Felhunter's Tainted Blood, the healing of its Devour Magic, and the detection level of its Paranoia by 10%, and reduces the cooldown of its Spell Lock by 2 sec.

  • Demonic Knowledge Demonology 20 points 3 ranks

    Increases your spell damage and your Demon pet's spell damage by up to 33% of your level while you have a summoned Demon pet active.

  • Demonic Pact Demonology 30 points 1 rank

    Your Demonic Sacrifice effect is no longer cancelled by summoning a different Demon pet. Resummoning the sacrificed pet will still cancel the effect.

  • Molten Skin Destruction 5 points 5 ranks

    Reduces all damage taken by 2%.

  • Bane of Havoc Destruction 20 points 1 rank

    Afflicts the target for 5 min, causing 15% of all damage done by the Warlock to other targets to also be dealt to the cursed target. Bane of Havoc is limited to 1 target, and only one Bane per Warlock can be active on any one target.

  • Fire and Brimstone Destruction 20 points 3 ranks

    Increases the critical strike chance of your Conflagrate spell by 8%.

  • Shadow and Flame Destruction 25 points 5 ranks

    Hitting an enemy with Conflagrate increases all Shadow damage you deal by 2% for 20 sec, and hitting an enemy with Shadowburn increases all Fire damage you deal by 2% for 20 sec. In addition, Conflagrate has a 20% chance not to consume Immolate, and Shadowburn has a 20% chance to instantly refund a Soul Shard.

  • Incinerate Destruction 30 points 1 rank

    Deals 99 to 115 Fire damage to your target and an additional 25% damage if the target is afflicted by Immolate.

Questions about Warlock talent changes

How many Warlock talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 50 Warlock talents in Classic Era, 7 read exactly the same in Forever, 28 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 15 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 17 new Warlock talents, for 52 in all.
What are the new Warlock talents in WoW Forever?
17 Warlock talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Soul Harvest, Improved Drains, Pandemic, Malevolence, Wrack, Demonic Aegis, Demonic Energies, Decimation, Demonic Brand, Improved Felhunter, Demonic Knowledge, Demonic Pact, Molten Skin, Bane of Havoc, Fire and Brimstone, Shadow and Flame and Incinerate.
Which Classic Warlock talents are not in WoW Forever?
15 Classic Era Warlock talents are not in the Forever trees: Dark Pact, Improved Curse of Exhaustion, Improved Curse of Weakness, Improved Drain Mana, Improved Drain Soul, Fel Stamina, Improved Firestone, Improved Healthstone, Improved Spellstone, Improved Subjugate Demon, Devastation, Emberstorm, Improved Firebolt, Improved Immolate and Improved Lash of Pain. Dark Pact is taught by trainers instead.
Which Warlock tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Demonology: 7 new talents, 9 changed and 5 gone from the tree, against 3 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Warlock talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
4 Warlock talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Improved Bane of Agony (was Improved Curse of Agony), Soul Siphon (was Improved Drain Life), Fel Vitality (was Fel Intellect) and Agonizing Flames (was Improved Searing Pain).
