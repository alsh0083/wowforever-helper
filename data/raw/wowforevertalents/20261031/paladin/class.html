Paladin talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Paladin 44 talents. Forever keeps 31 of them (7 word for word, 19 with a rewritten tooltip and 5 with the same sentence and different numbers) and renames 1, changes the rank count of 3, adds 20 new talents and leaves 13 out of the trees (2 of those are taught by trainers instead). Holy moved most: 9 new, 6 changed, 4 gone and 2 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Holy 9 new · 6 changed · 2 same · 4 gone
  • Protection 6 new · 8 changed · 3 same · 5 gone
  • Retribution 5 new · 10 changed · 2 same · 4 gone

New Paladin talents in Forever (20)

  • Reverence Holy 5 points 2 ranks

    Allows 15% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

  • Purifying Power Holy 5 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the mana cost of your Cleanse and Purify spells by 10% and reduces the cooldown of your Exorcism and Holy Wrath spells by 17%.

  • Holy Conduit Holy 10 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the mana cost of your Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism, and Hammer of Wrath spells by 20%.

  • Divine Precision Holy 15 points 2 ranks

    Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 4%, and reduces the duration of Silence effects on you by 10%.

  • Infusion of Light Holy 15 points 2 ranks

    Your Holy Shock and Flash of Light critical hits reduce the cast time of your next Holy Light cast within 15 sec by 0.5 sec.

  • Oath of Light Holy 15 points 1 rank

    Swear an Oath to protect target ally for 5 min, increasing the healing of your Holy Light on them by up to 400, your Flash of Light by up to 115, and your Holy Shock by up to 230. Only one Oath of Light may be sworn by the Paladin at any one time.

  • Consecrated Ground Holy 20 points 2 ranks

    Gives your Holy spells 5% increased damage against the first 4 enemies that enter your Consecration.

  • Boundless Light Holy 20 points 2 ranks

    Increases the critical strike chance of your Holy Shock spell by 15% when cast on allies not affected by your Oath of Light.

  • Light's Vigil Holy 30 points 1 rank

    Applies Light's Vigil to a friendly target for 30 sec. The caster's next Holy Shock on the target incurs no cooldown and heals the target and their party for 325 to 344. Only one Vigil per Paladin may be active in each party.

  • Sacred Duty Protection 5 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the cooldown of your Divine Shield, Divine Protection, and Templar's Bulwark spells by 30 sec, and reduces the duration of Forbearance by 5 sec.

  • Templar's Bulwark Protection 10 points 1 rank

    When activated, this ability grants you an absorb shield equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 sec. Applies Forbearance for 1 min. Cannot be cast while Forbearance is active.

  • Swift Judgement Protection 15 points 1 rank

    Unleash the energy of a Seal spell upon an enemy. Does not consume the Seal. Refer to individual Seals for Judgement effect. Does not share a cooldown with your Judgement ability.

  • Stoicism Protection 20 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the duration of Stun and Charm effects on you by 10%.

  • Iron Creed Protection 25 points 3 ranks

    Increases the threat generated by your Holy Strike ability by 10%. While Righteous Fury is active, Holy Strike also reduces your damage taken by 2% for 6 sec.

  • Shieldsworn Protection 30 points 1 rank

    Reduces the cooldown of your Holy Strike ability by 4 sec. Additionally, Holy Strike now strikes with your shield, triggering your active Holy Shield and Shield Specialization talents without consuming their charges or triggering their cooldowns, and allowing you to Block spells for 6 sec.

  • Sanctified Judgement Retribution 5 points 3 ranks

    Gives your Judgement ability a 33% chance to return 20% of the Mana cost of the judged seal.

  • Sacred Arbiter Retribution 15 points 1 rank

    Increases the damage of your Holy Strike ability by 20% and causes it to refresh all Judgement effects on the target.

  • Turn Evil Retribution 20 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the cast time of your Turn Undead spell by 0.5 sec, its cooldown by 5 sec, and allows it to affect Demons.

  • Champion of the Light Retribution 25 points 5 ranks

    Increases your spell damage by up to 12% of your Intellect, and reduces the cast time of your Hammer of Wrath by 0.2 sec.

  • Unsealed Wrath Retribution 30 points 1 rank

    Your melee attacks store a copy of your active Seal for 15 sec, stacking up to 3 times. Landing an attack with a different Seal consumes all stacks, giving the stored Seal its normal chance to activate once per stack.

Questions about Paladin talent changes

How many Paladin talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 44 Paladin talents in Classic Era, 7 read exactly the same in Forever, 24 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 13 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 20 new Paladin talents, for 51 in all.
What are the new Paladin talents in WoW Forever?
20 Paladin talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Reverence, Purifying Power, Holy Conduit, Divine Precision, Infusion of Light, Oath of Light, Consecrated Ground, Boundless Light, Light's Vigil, Sacred Duty, Templar's Bulwark, Swift Judgement, Stoicism, Iron Creed, Shieldsworn, Sanctified Judgement, Sacred Arbiter, Turn Evil, Champion of the Light and Unsealed Wrath.
Which Classic Paladin talents are not in WoW Forever?
13 Classic Era Paladin talents are not in the Forever trees: Consecration, Improved Blessing of Wisdom, Improved Lay on Hands, Lasting Judgement, Blessing of Kings, Blessing of Sanctuary, Improved Concentration Aura, Improved Devotion Aura, One-Handed Weapon Specialization, Improved Blessing of Might, Improved Retribution Aura, Improved Seal of the Crusader and Sanctity Aura. Consecration and Blessing of Sanctuary are taught by trainers instead.
Which Paladin tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Holy: 9 new talents, 6 changed and 4 gone from the tree, against 2 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Paladin talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
1 Paladin talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Improved Seals (was Improved Seal of Righteousness).
