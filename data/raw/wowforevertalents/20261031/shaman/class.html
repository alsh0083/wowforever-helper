Shaman talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Shaman 46 talents. Forever keeps 38 of them (8 word for word, 25 with a rewritten tooltip and 5 with the same sentence and different numbers) and renames 5, changes the rank count of 4, adds 12 new talents and leaves 8 out of the trees. Enhancement moved most: 6 new, 9 changed, 5 gone and 3 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Elemental Combat 3 new · 11 changed · 2 same · 1 gone
  • Enhancement 6 new · 9 changed · 3 same · 5 gone
  • Restoration 3 new · 10 changed · 3 same · 2 gone

New Shaman talents in Forever (12)

  • Earthbound Elemental Combat 15 points 1 rank

    Your Earthbind Totem Immobilizes nearby targets for 5 sec when cast.

  • Lava Burst Elemental Combat 20 points 1 rank

    You hurl molten lava at the target, dealing 113 to 146 Fire damage. If your Flame Shock is on the target, Lava Burst deals 20% increased damage.

  • Lightning Overload Elemental Combat 20 points 3 ranks

    Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 3% chance to cast a second, similar spell on the same target at no additional cost that causes half damage and no threat.

  • Mental Dexterity Enhancement 5 points 3 ranks

    Increases your Attack Power by an amount equal to 33% of your Intellect.

  • Shamanistic Focus Enhancement 10 points 1 rank

    Reduces the mana cost of your Shock and Lightning Shield spells by 45%.

  • Mental Quickness Enhancement 20 points 2 ranks

    Increases your spell damage and healing by up to 15% of your Intellect.

  • Improved Stormstrike Enhancement 20 points 2 ranks

    When you Stormstrike, you have a 50% chance to gain 50% mana regeneration while casting spells for 15 sec, and Stormstrike's cooldown has a 50% chance to reset each time you Dodge or Parry.

  • Maelstrom Weapon Enhancement 25 points 5 ranks

    When you deal damage with a melee attack, you have a chance to reduce the cast time and Mana cost of your next Lightning Bolt spell by 4%. Stacks up to 5 times. Lasts 30 sec.

  • Rage of the Farseer Enhancement 30 points 1 rank

    Increases your attack speed by 30% for 25 sec.

  • Mindfulness Restoration 5 points 3 ranks

    Allows 17% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

  • Water Shield Restoration 10 points 1 rank

    The caster is surrounded by 3 globes of water. When a spell, melee, or ranged attack hits the caster or when one of the caster's spells gets a critical result, 2% of maximum mana is restored to the caster, expending one water globe. Only one globe will activate every few seconds. Lasts 10 min. Only one Elemental Shield can be active on the Shaman at any one time.

  • Riptide Restoration 30 points 1 rank

    Heals a friendly target for 484 to 535, an additional 445 over 15 sec, and increases the effectiveness of your Chain Heal casts directly on that target by 25%.

Questions about Shaman talent changes

How many Shaman talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 46 Shaman talents in Classic Era, 8 read exactly the same in Forever, 30 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 8 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 12 new Shaman talents, for 50 in all.
What are the new Shaman talents in WoW Forever?
12 Shaman talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Earthbound, Lava Burst, Lightning Overload, Mental Dexterity, Shamanistic Focus, Mental Quickness, Improved Stormstrike, Maelstrom Weapon, Rage of the Farseer, Mindfulness, Water Shield and Riptide.
Which Classic Shaman talents are not in WoW Forever?
8 Classic Era Shaman talents are not in the Forever trees: Call of Thunder, Enhancing Totems, Improved Weapon Totems, Shield Specialization, Two-Handed Axes and Maces, Weapon Mastery, Nature's Guidance and Totemic Mastery.
Which Shaman tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Enhancement: 6 new talents, 9 changed and 5 gone from the tree, against 3 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Shaman talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
5 Shaman talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Elemental Alacrity (was Lightning Mastery), Trial by Fire (was Improved Fire Totems), Elemental Reach (was Storm Reach), Spirit Weapons (was Parry) and Natural Grace (was Healing Grace).

    Rank 1 Increases the health of your Stoneclaw Totem by 25% and the radius of your Earthbind Totem by 10%.

    Rank 2 Increases the health of your Stoneclaw Totem by 50% and the radius of your Earthbind Totem by 20%.

  • Thundering Strikes 5 ranks ◆

    Rank 1 Improves your chance to get a critical strike with all spells and attacks by 1%.

    Rank 5 Improves your chance to get a critical strike with all spells and attacks by 5%.

    Classic rank 1 Improves your chance to get a critical strike with your weapon attacks by 1%.

    Classic rank 5 Improves your chance to get a critical strike with your weapon attacks by 5%.

  • Ancestral Knowledge 5 ranks ◆

    Rank 1 Increases your Intellect by 2%.

    Rank 5 Increases your Intellect by 10%.

    Classic rank 1 Increases your maximum Mana by 1%.

    Classic rank 5 Increases your maximum Mana by 5%.

  • Guardian Totems 2 ranks 5 points

    Rank 1 Increases the amount of damage reduced by your Stoneskin Totem and Windwall Totem by 10% and reduces the cooldown of your Grounding Totem by 1 sec.

    Rank 2 Increases the amount of damage reduced by your Stoneskin Totem and Windwall Totem by 20% and reduces the cooldown of your Grounding Totem by 2 sec.

  • Mental Dexterity 3 ranks 5 points ★

    Rank 1 Increases your Attack Power by an amount equal to 33% of your Intellect.

    Rank 3 Increases your Attack Power by an amount equal to 100% of your Intellect.

  • Improved Ghost Wolf 2 ranks 5 points ◆

    Rank 1 Reduces the cast time of your Ghost Wolf spell by 1 sec, and Ghost Wolf may be used indoors.

    Rank 2 Reduces the cast time of your Ghost Wolf spell by 3 sec, and Ghost Wolf may be used indoors.

    Classic rank 1 Reduces the cast time of your Ghost Wolf spell by 1 sec.

    Classic rank 2 Reduces the cast time of your Ghost Wolf spell by 2 sec.

  • Improved Lightning Shield 3 ranks 5 points

    Rank 1 Increases the damage done by your Lightning Shield orbs by 5%.

    Rank 3 Increases the damage done by your Lightning Shield orbs by 15%.

  • Elemental Weapons 3 ranks 10 points ◆

    Rank 1 Increases the melee attack power bonus of your Rockbiter Weapon by 7%, your Windfury Weapon effect by 13% and increases the damage caused by your Flametongue Weapon and Frostbrand Weapon by 5%.

    Rank 3 Increases the melee attack power bonus of your Rockbiter Weapon by 20%, your Windfury Weapon effect by 40% and increases the damage caused by your Flametongue Weapon and Frostbrand Weapon by 15%.

  • Shamanistic Focus 1 rank 10 points ★

    Reduces the mana cost of your Shock and Lightning Shield spells by 45%.

  • Anticipation 3 ranks 10 points ◆

    Rank 1 Increases your chance to dodge by an additional 2%.

    Rank 3 Increases your chance to dodge by an additional 6%.

    Classic rank 3 Increases your chance to dodge by an additional 3%.

  • Toughness 5 ranks 15 points ◆

    Rank 1 Increases your Stamina by 2%.

    Rank 5 Increases your Stamina by 10%.

    Classic rank 1 Increases your armor value from items by 2%.

    Classic rank 5 Increases your armor value from items by 10%.

  • Flurry 5 ranks 15 points ◆

    Rank 1 Increases your attack speed by 5% for your next 3 swings after dealing a melee critical strike.

    Rank 5 Increases your attack speed by 25% for your next 3 swings after dealing a melee critical strike.

    Classic rank 1 Increases your attack speed by 10% for your next 3 swings after dealing a critical strike.

    Classic rank 5 Increases your attack speed by 30% for your next 3 swings after dealing a critical strike.

  • Stormstrike 1 rank 15 points ◆

    8% of base mana · Melee Range · Instant · 8 sec cooldown

    Instantly strike for normal weapon damage and increase the damage you deal to the target with your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, or Earth Shock spell by 20% for 12 sec.

    Classic Gives you an extra attack. In addition, the next 2 sources of Nature damage dealt to the target are increased by 20%. Lasts 12 sec.

  • Spirit Weapons 1 rank 20 points ◆

    Gives a chance to parry enemy melee attacks, reduces all threat generated by your attacks by 30% while Rockbiter Weapon is not active, and increases all threat generated by 30% while Rockbiter Weapon is active.

    Classic Was Parry.

    Classic Gives a chance to parry enemy melee attacks.

  • Mental Quickness 2 ranks 20 points ★

    Rank 1 Increases your spell damage and healing by up to 15% of your Intellect.

    Rank 2 Increases your spell damage and healing by up to 30% of your Intellect.

  • Improved Stormstrike 2 ranks 20 points ★

    Rank 1 When you Stormstrike, you have a 50% chance to gain 50% mana regeneration while casting spells for 15 sec, and Stormstrike's cooldown has a 50% chance to reset each time you Dodge or Parry.

    Rank 2 When you Stormstrike, you have a 100% chance to gain 50% mana regeneration while casting spells for 15 sec, and Stormstrike's cooldown has a 100% chance to reset each time you Dodge or Parry.

  • Maelstrom Weapon 5 ranks 25 points ★

    Rank 1 When you deal damage with a melee attack, you have a chance to reduce the cast time and Mana cost of your next Lightning Bolt spell by 4%. Stacks up to 5 times. Lasts 30 sec.

    Rank 5 When you deal damage with a melee attack, you have a chance to reduce the cast time and Mana cost of your next Lightning Bolt spell by 20%. Stacks up to 5 times. Lasts 30 sec.

  • Rage of the Farseer 1 rank 30 points ★

    Instant · 3 min cooldown

    Increases your attack speed by 30% for 25 sec.

About the Shaman data

All 50 Shaman talents are read from the World of Warcraft: Forever client itself: their position, rank count, prerequisites and tooltip text (12 new to Forever, 30 changed from Classic, 8 word-for-word the same; 8 Classic Shaman talents are not in the Forever trees at all). Nothing here is a Classic placeholder or a guess from footage. Show original Classic does not change the tree: hover a talent to read the Classic Era tooltip under the Forever one.
