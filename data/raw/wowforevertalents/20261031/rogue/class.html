Rogue talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Rogue 51 talents. Forever keeps 44 of them (9 word for word, 27 with a rewritten tooltip, 4 with the same sentence and different numbers and 4 differing only in capitalisation or punctuation) and renames 1, changes the rank count of 6, adds 9 new talents and leaves 7 out of the trees. Subtlety moved most: 5 new, 12 changed, 3 gone and 2 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Assassination 2 new · 13 changed · 2 same · 0 gone
  • Combat 2 new · 10 changed · 5 same · 4 gone
  • Subtlety 5 new · 12 changed · 2 same · 3 gone

New Rogue talents in Forever (9)

  • Mutilate Assassination 20 points 1 rank

    Instantly attacks with both weapons for 75% weapon damage plus an additional 17.25 with each weapon. Damage increased by 20% against Poisoned targets. Awards 2 Combo Points.

  • Venom Assassination 30 points 1 rank

    Finishing move that increases the damage of your Poisons by 30% and your chance to apply Poisons by 10%. Lasts longer per combo point: 1 point : 9 sec 2 points: 12 sec 3 points: 15 sec 4 points: 18 sec 5 points: 21 sec

  • Puncturing Wounds Combat 5 points 3 ranks

    Increases the critical strike chance of your Backstab by 10% and your Mutilate by 5%, and gives Backstab a 15% chance to add an additional Combo Point.

  • Flawless Execution Combat 15 points 1 rank

    Reduces the Energy cost of your Eviscerate ability by 10.

  • Dirty Tricks Subtlety 5 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the Energy cost of your Sap and Blind abilities by 25%.

  • Improved Distract Subtlety 10 points 2 ranks

    Increases the radius of your Distract ability by 3 yds, and further reduces the Stealth detection of distracted enemies as though they were an additional 1 levels lower.

  • Quietus Subtlety 25 points 5 ranks

    Your Sinister Strike, Ghostly Strike, and Hemorrhage abilities cause 2% more damage against targets below 35% health.

  • Cutthroat Subtlety 25 points 5 ranks

    Your Backstab has a 3% chance to cause your next Ambush within 10 sec to not require Stealth.

  • Thousand Cuts Subtlety 30 points 1 rank

    When your Rupture ability deals periodic damage, the Energy cost of your next Hemorrhage or Backstab ability within 10 sec is reduced by 3, stacking up to 5 times.

Questions about Rogue talent changes

How many Rogue talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 51 Rogue talents in Classic Era, 9 read exactly the same in Forever, 35 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 7 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 9 new Rogue talents, for 53 in all.
What are the new Rogue talents in WoW Forever?
9 Rogue talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Mutilate, Venom, Puncturing Wounds, Flawless Execution, Dirty Tricks, Improved Distract, Quietus, Cutthroat and Thousand Cuts.
Which Classic Rogue talents are not in WoW Forever?
7 Classic Era Rogue talents are not in the Forever trees: Dagger Specialization, Fist Weapon Specialization, Improved Backstab, Mace Specialization, Deadliness, Improved Sap and Sleight of Hand.
Which Rogue tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Subtlety: 5 new talents, 12 changed and 3 gone from the tree, against 2 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Rogue talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
1 Rogue talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Hack and Slash (was Sword Specialization).
