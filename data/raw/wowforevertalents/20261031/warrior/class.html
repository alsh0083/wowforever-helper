Warrior talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Warrior 52 talents. Forever keeps 42 of them (7 word for word, 30 with a rewritten tooltip, 3 with the same sentence and different numbers and 1 differing only in capitalisation or punctuation) and renames 2, changes the rank count of 3, adds 10 new talents and leaves 10 out of the trees. Protection moved most: 3 new, 15 changed, 3 gone and 0 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Arms 3 new · 9 changed · 5 same · 4 gone
  • Fury 4 new · 11 changed · 2 same · 3 gone
  • Protection 3 new · 15 changed · 0 same · 3 gone

New Warrior talents in Forever (10)

  • Spearing Strike Arms 15 points 1 rank

    A brutal attack that deals 40% weapon damage. Deals an additional 80% weapon damage against Giants, Dragonkin, and mounted targets. Mounted targets are dismounted.

  • Bloodthrill Arms 20 points 5 ranks

    Grants your Main Hand melee attacks against enemies afflicted by your Rend a 4% chance to allow an activation of Overpower.

  • Weaponmaster Arms 20 points 5 ranks

    Gives your melee weapon attacks a benefit depending on the weapon. Axe/Polearm: Increases your critical strike chance by 1%. Mace/Staff: Your attacks ignore 3% of your target's armor. Sword: Your successful melee attacks have a 1% chance to trigger an extra attack on the target.

  • Lingering Rage Fury 5 points 5 ranks

    Increases the time before your Rage begins to decay after leaving combat by 2 sec.

  • Furious Precision Fury 10 points 3 ranks

    Increases your chance to hit with off-hand attacks by 4%.

  • Raging Blows Fury 15 points 1 rank

    Reduces the Rage cost of your Cleave and Whirlwind abilities by 3.

  • Gore Drinker Fury 25 points 2 ranks

    Your Enrage, Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish, and Bloodthirst abilities cause your next 3 melee attacks to restore 0.5% of your maximum Health.

  • Master of Defense Protection 10 points 2 ranks

    Grants you a 50% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Dodge or Parry while a shield is equipped.

  • Vanguard Protection 15 points 1 rank

    Your Charge ability is now usable while in Defensive Stance.

  • Focused Rage Protection 20 points 3 ranks

    Reduces the Rage cost of your offensive abilities by 1.

Questions about Warrior talent changes

How many Warrior talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 52 Warrior talents in Classic Era, 7 read exactly the same in Forever, 35 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 10 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 10 new Warrior talents, for 52 in all.
What are the new Warrior talents in WoW Forever?
10 Warrior talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Spearing Strike, Bloodthrill, Weaponmaster, Lingering Rage, Furious Precision, Raging Blows, Gore Drinker, Master of Defense, Vanguard and Focused Rage.
Which Classic Warrior talents are not in WoW Forever?
10 Classic Era Warrior talents are not in the Forever trees: Axe Specialization, Mace Specialization, Polearm Specialization, Sword Specialization, Improved Battle Shout, Improved Cleave, Improved Demoralizing Shout, Improved Shield Block, Improved Taunt and Toughness.
Which Warrior tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Protection: 3 new talents, 15 changed and 3 gone from the tree, against 0 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Warrior talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
2 Warrior talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Improved Tactical Mastery (was Tactical Mastery) and Bastion (was One-Handed Weapon Specialization).
