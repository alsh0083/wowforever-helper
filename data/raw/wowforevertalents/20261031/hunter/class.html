Hunter talent changes from Classic to Forever

Classic Era gave the Hunter 46 talents. Forever keeps 37 of them (12 word for word, 22 with a rewritten tooltip and 3 with the same sentence and different numbers) and renames 4, changes the rank count of 3, adds 14 new talents and leaves 9 out of the trees (2 of those are taught by trainers instead). Survival moved most: 6 new, 9 changed, 4 gone and 3 left exactly as in Classic. Each line below was read from the two clients and compared rank for rank; nothing is taken from patch notes.

  • Beast Mastery 2 new · 10 changed · 4 same · 2 gone
  • Marksmanship 6 new · 6 changed · 5 same · 3 gone
  • Survival 6 new · 9 changed · 3 same · 4 gone

New Hunter talents in Forever (14)

  • Focused Fire Beast Mastery 5 points 2 ranks

    Increases all damage you and your pet deal by 1% while your pet is active.

  • Summon Hawk Beast Mastery 15 points 1 rank

    Command a hawk to dive-bomb your targeted enemy, dealing (32 + 5% of Ranged Attack Power) Physical damage and continuing its assault for 18 sec. Only 2 hawks can be active at once. Summon Hawk shares its cooldown with Arcane Shot.

  • Improved Stings Marksmanship 5 points 3 ranks

    Increases the damage of your Serpent Sting ability by 6%, reduces the cooldown of your Viper Sting ability by 2 sec, and increases the duration of your Scorpid Sting ability by 15 sec.

  • Careful Aim Marksmanship 5 points 5 ranks

    Increases your Attack Power by 20% of your Intellect.

  • Rapid Killing Marksmanship 10 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the cooldown on your Rapid Fire ability by 1 min. In addition, when you kill a non-trivial enemy or it dies while afflicted by your Serpent Sting, you gain Rapid Killing, increasing the damage of your next Shot ability within 20 sec by 10%.

  • Lone Wolf Marksmanship 10 points 1 rank

    You deal 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.

  • Rapid Recuperation Marksmanship 20 points 2 ranks

    Afflicting a target with Serpent Sting allows 25% of your Mana Regeneration to continue while casting for 15 sec, increased to 50% when Rapid Killing is consumed.

  • Sniper Shot Marksmanship 30 points 1 rank

    A long-range shot that deals ranged damage plus 160 and increases the range of your next 3 Shots by 10 yards for 10 sec.

  • Predator's Edge Survival 15 points 5 ranks

    Increases your melee ability critical strike damage by 6% and your Off Hand weapon damage by 10%.

  • Resourcefulness Survival 20 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the mana cost of your Trap abilities and melee abilities by 30%. In addition, your critical strikes have a 50% chance to allow 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting for 30 sec.

  • Expose Prey Survival 20 points 2 ranks

    Grants your melee and ranged attacks against enemies afflicted by Hunter's Mark a 5% chance to allow an activation of Mongoose Bite. Lasts 10 sec.

  • Survivalist's Discipline Survival 20 points 2 ranks

    Reduces the cooldown of your Trap and Deterrence abilities by 20%.

  • Strider Kick Survival 20 points 1 rank

    A powerful kick that deals 100% melee weapon damage and increases movement speed by 30% for 3 sec.

  • Lacerating Strikes Survival 30 points 1 rank

    Your Mongoose Bite also causes the target to Bleed for damage equal to 40% of the damage done by Mongoose Bite over 21 sec

Questions about Hunter talent changes

How many Hunter talents changed from Classic in WoW Forever?
Of the 46 Hunter talents in Classic Era, 12 read exactly the same in Forever, 25 have a different tooltip, name or rank count, and 9 are not in the Forever trees. Forever adds 14 new Hunter talents, for 51 in all.
What are the new Hunter talents in WoW Forever?
14 Hunter talents have no counterpart in Classic Era: Focused Fire, Summon Hawk, Improved Stings, Careful Aim, Rapid Killing, Lone Wolf, Rapid Recuperation, Sniper Shot, Predator's Edge, Resourcefulness, Expose Prey, Survivalist's Discipline, Strider Kick and Lacerating Strikes.
Which Classic Hunter talents are not in WoW Forever?
9 Classic Era Hunter talents are not in the Forever trees: Improved Eyes of the Beast, Thick Hide, Aimed Shot, Improved Hunter's Mark, Improved Scorpid Sting, Humanoid Slaying, Improved Feign Death, Killer Instinct and Wyvern Sting. Aimed Shot and Wyvern Sting are taught by trainers instead.
Which Hunter tree changed the most in WoW Forever?
Survival: 6 new talents, 9 changed and 4 gone from the tree, against 3 left exactly as in Classic.
Which Hunter talents were renamed in WoW Forever?
4 Hunter talents keep their Classic spell under a new name: Deadly Aspects (was Improved Aspect of the Hawk), Lethal Attacks (was Lethal Shots), Improved Tracking (was Monster Slaying) and Survival Tactics (was Trap Mastery).
