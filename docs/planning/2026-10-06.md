# Planning round, 2026-10-06: recommended gear (milestone gear)

Owner idea: suggest gear upgrades and where to get them, respecting Horde and Alliance. Decisions:

- **Q1, character data:** an official armory will come; until then there's no import. The gear panel follows the journey's level: as you level up and spend talent points, it shows the recommended gear for that level. Armory import later (#191).
- **Q2, ranking:** the build's own engine score (stat weights per build and level, #189); healer and tank builds use generic weights.
- **Q3, sources:** every item shows its source (boss drop, quest and so on). Hard-to-get items are excluded: rare-mob drops and rare bind-on-equip world drops.
- **Q4, faction:** a Horde/Alliance toggle next to the class tabs, remembered in the browser.
- **Q5, other sources:** vendor and crafted gear appear as an alternative recommendation, tagged "vendor" or "crafted (profession)"; random world drops are excluded.
- **Q6, panel:** per slot, the best item usable at that level plus one or two runner-ups, each with its gain for the selected build and its source; dungeon items only within the dungeon's level range. With an armory import, the panel targets the optimal upgrade over what you wear.
- **Q7, timing:** build now, labeled "beta data". Blizzard deliberately makes the full item list hard to datamine, so the item data needs refreshing as more gets recorded: a refresh command run on demand (no scheduled services), items missing from the client tables fall back to the site's item records, and the panel shows when item data was last checked.
