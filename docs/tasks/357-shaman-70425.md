## Why
Patch 1.60.1.70425 rebuilt the Elemental tree. `TALENT_EFFECTS` rules read their numbers from the rank text
(`effects.py`: one value per rank, `sign * combine(match)` when a rule has `combine`).
- **Call of Thunder** is gone from the data.
- **Improved Fire Nova** is renamed **Trial by Fire**. Its new text also lengthens Magma Totem; it stays unmodeled.
- **Elemental Mastery** is back (1 rank): "Gain mastery over Fire and Lightning, reducing the cast time of
  Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst by 0.5 sec, and removing the cooldown from Chain Lightning. Lasts 15 sec."
  Its cooldown is 180 s (wago SpellCooldowns, spell 16166; not in the rank text). Best effort: a cast-time cut of
  0.5 s times its uptime, 15 / 180. Chain Lightning's lost cooldown is not modeled.

## Steps

`src/wowforever/classes/shaman.py`:
1. Delete the `"Call of Thunder"` entry from `TALENT_EFFECTS`.
2. Add an `"Elemental Mastery"` entry to `TALENT_EFFECTS`, after `"Lightning Overload"`: one `EffectRule` of kind
   `"cast_time"`, applies_to `("Lightning Bolt", "Lava Burst")`, `sign=-1`. Its pattern captures the seconds
   ("Lava Burst by (number) sec") and the duration ("Lasts (number) sec"). Its `combine` returns
   seconds × duration / 180. Put a module-level constant `ELEMENTAL_MASTERY_COOLDOWN = 180.0` with a comment
   naming the source (wago SpellCooldowns, spell 16166) above `TALENT_EFFECTS`, and use it in `combine`. Put a
   comment line above the rule: uptime-weighted, and Chain Lightning's cooldown removal isn't modeled.
3. `UNMODELED`: replace the `"Improved Fire Nova"` line with
   `"Trial by Fire": "damage: Fire Nova damage and cooldown, longer Magma Totem",`.
