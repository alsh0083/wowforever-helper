## Why
Patch 1.60.1.70425 changed these priest talents. `TALENT_EFFECTS` rules read their numbers from the rank text
(`effects.py` turns each regex match into one value per rank; `combine` gets the match object).
- **Shadow Weaving:** "...have a 100% chance to increase the Shadow damage the target takes from you by 2% for 15
  sec, stacking up to 5 times." Before, it said "the Shadow damage you deal". For one priest it's the same number.
- **Improved Mind Flay** (2 ranks): "Each instance of damage of Mind Flay deals 10%/20% more damage than its
  previous instance of damage." Mind Flay has 3 ticks (3 s channel, 1 s period in the data). With x the
  percentage as a fraction, the mean tick is ((1+x)^3 - 1) / (3x) times the first, so the bonus is that minus 1,
  times 100.
- **Improved Mind Blast** (now the 5-rank Shadow row-2 talent): "Reduces the cooldown of your Mind Blast spell by
  0.5 ... 2.5 sec." The existing rule already reads it.
- **Mental Strength** (5 ranks): "Increases your total Intellect by 2% ... 10%."
- **Dark Inspiration** (new, 5 ranks): "Your Mind Blast and fully channeled Mind Flay causes Dark Inspiration,
  increasing yours and your party's Spirit by 3% ... 15% for 10 sec." Mind Blast every 5.5-8 s keeps it up, so
  the model counts full uptime (best effort; say so in a comment).
- **Shadow Affinity** and **Improved Fade** are no longer talents in the data.

## Steps, in this order

### 1. `src/wowforever/classes/priest.py`
- `"Shadow Weaving"`: the pattern accepts both texts, "you deal" or "the target takes from you", using a
  non-capturing group so the `combine` groups stay 1, 2 and 3.
- `"Improved Mind Flay"`: the pattern matches "Mind Flay deals N% more damage than its previous instance". Add a
  `combine` that returns the mean-tick bonus above, as a percentage. Put a comment line above the rule saying
  why (3 ticks, each x% more).
- Add `"Mental Strength"` (kind `"stat_pct"`, applies_to `("intellect",)`) and `"Dark Inspiration"` (kind
  `"stat_pct"`, applies_to `("spirit",)`) to `TALENT_EFFECTS`, before `"Meditation"`, each reading its
  percentage. Add a comment line above Dark Inspiration: full uptime assumed.
- `UNMODELED`: remove `"Mental Strength"`, `"Shadow Affinity"` and `"Improved Fade"`.

### 2. `src/wowforever/caster.py`
- Add `def talent_stats(class_name: str, stats: Stats, cls: ClassData, ranks: Mapping[int, int]) -> Stats:`
  just before `caster_rotation`. For every talent the build takes (`ranks.get(talent_id, 0) > 0`), for each of
  its effects with kind `"stat_pct"`: the stat is `applies_to[0]`. Add `getattr(stats, stat) * value / 100` of
  it with `wowforever.stat_weights.apply(class_name, stats, stat, amount)` (import it inside the function).
  `apply` returns `None` when the stat does nothing; then keep `stats`. `value` is the effect's value at the
  build's rank (`values[rank - 1]`). Return the new stats; with no such talents, return `stats` unchanged.
  Docstring: the build's `stat_pct` talents (Mental Strength, Dark Inspiration; 70425, #356) through the class's
  conversions.
- `caster_rotation`: as its first statement after the docstring (before `plan = ...`), set
  `stats = talent_stats(class_name, stats, cls, ranks)`.
