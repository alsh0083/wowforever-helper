## Why
Fury warriors dual wield and use Bloodthirst, but the rotation swings one two-hander. With an off hand: off-hand
swings at half damage with the dual-wield miss penalty, Dual Wield Specialization (+25% off-hand damage, +50%
off-hand Rage), Furious Precision (+10% off-hand hit), Bloodthirst (its base damage plus 45% of attack power:
SpellEffect 23894 effect 1, `$s2`), and Two-Handed Weapon Specialization no longer applies. Fury builds (main tree
Fury) take the `warrior_dw` table's weapons, keeping the rest of their stats.
