## Why
Shank returns more Energy than it costs on a 10 s cooldown, so leaving it out understated every rogue spec by about
a quarter (#470). The rotation casts it on cooldown with an off-hand weapon:
- its 50% off-hand strike;
- its net Energy;
- Boot Knife's 2 Combo Points, which shorten the finisher cycle;
- Venom's poison buff for 6 s of every cooldown.

Flash of Steel, Boot Knife and Venom move from UNMODELED to rules. Composure stays out: Poised works only out of
combat, once a pull.
